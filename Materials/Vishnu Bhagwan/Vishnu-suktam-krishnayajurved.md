Vishnu SUktam \[Krishna Yajur ved\]

Vishnu Suktam \- Introduction

The **Vishnu Suktam** presented here is drawn from the **Taittirīya Saṃhitā** of the Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda, one of the most ancient Vedic texts glorifying **Lord Vishnu** as the cosmic sustainer. It should be noted that a separate Rig Vedic Vishnu Suktam also exists; this is the Yajurvedic recension widely used in South Indian temple and ritual traditions.

The hymn opens with a Vedic ṛk verse proclaiming Vishnu's heroic deeds — how he measured the earthly realms and propped up the highest celestial abode in three great strides, earning him the epithet *Urugāya*, the wide-going one. This is followed by a sacred Yajus formula in which ritual offerings are consecrated by identifying them with Vishnu himself — his sovereignty, his support, his steadfastness. Recited during Vaishnava rituals and daily worship, regular recitation is said to bring Vishnu's protection and draw the devotee toward his supreme abode.

Translation

*Om\! I shall now proclaim the heroic deeds of Vishnu, who measured out the earthly realms, who established the higher celestial abode, striding forth in three steps, the wide-going One. You are the sovereignty of Vishnu, you are the back (support) of Vishnu, you are the two corners of Vishnu's altar, you are the binding thread of Vishnu, you are the steadfast foundation of Vishnu, you belong to Vishnu — unto Vishnu I offer you.*

**तत्-विष्णोः (tat-viṣṇōḥ)** – that of Vishnu (tat:that | viṣṇōḥ:of Vishnu); **परमम्-पदम् (paramam-padam)** – supreme abode (paramam:supreme | padam:abode/station); **सदा (sadā)** – always; **पश्यन्ति (paśyanti)** – they behold; **सूरयः (sūrayaḥ)** – the wise seers; **दिवि-इव (divi-iva)** – like in heaven (divi:in heaven | iva:like); **चक्षुः (chakṣuḥ)** – eye; **आततम् (ātatam)** – stretched out, extended; **विप्रासः (viprāsaḥ)** – the illumined sages; **विपन्यवः (vipanyavaḥ)** – those who praise, hymn-singers; **जागृवान् (jāgṛvān)** – ever wakeful; **सम्-समिन्धते (sam-samiṃdhatē)** – they kindle together (sam:together | samiṃdhatē:they kindle); **पर्याप्त्या (paryāptyā)** – by complete attainment; **अनन्तरायाय (anantarāyāya)** – without obstruction; **सर्व-स्तोमः (sarva-stōmaḥ)** – the Sarva-stoma rite (sarva:all | stōmaḥ:hymn of praise); **अति-रात्रे (ati-rātrē)** – in the Atiratra ceremony (ati:beyond | rātrē:night); **उत्तम-महः (uttama-mahaḥ)** – supreme greatness (uttama:highest | mahaḥ:greatness); **भवति (bhavati)** – becomes; **सर्वस्य-आप्त्यै (sarvasya-āptyai)** – for obtaining all (sarvasya:of all | āptyai:for obtaining); **सर्वस्य-जित्त्यै (sarvasya-jittyai)** – for conquering all (sarvasya:of all | jittyai:for conquering); **सर्वम्-एव (sarvam-ēva)** – everything indeed (sarvam:all | ēva:indeed); **तेन-आप्नोति (tēna-āpnōti)** – by this one attains (tēna:by this | āpnōti:attains); **सर्वम्-जयति (sarvam-jayati)** – conquers all (sarvam:all | jayati:conquers);

ॐ शान्ति॒-श्शान्ति॒-श्शान्तिः॑ ॥

ōṃ śānti̠-śśānti̠-śśānti̍ḥ ॥

*Om. Peace, peace, peace — may there be tranquility in the three realms: the celestial, the atmospheric, and the terrestrial. May all disturbances born of divine forces, natural calamities, and one's own self be forever stilled.*

**ॐ (ōṃ)** – the sacred syllable Om; **शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः (śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ)** – peace, peace, peace (śāntiḥ:peace) — thrice uttered for peace from adhidaivika (divine), adhibhautika (natural), and adhyātmika (self-born) afflictions;
